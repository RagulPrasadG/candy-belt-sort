Privacy Policy for Candy Belt Sort

Last updated: 19 August 2026

PlayStoreGames (“we”) makes the Android game Candy Belt Sort. This policy explains what information the game uses.

Who this game is for

Candy Belt Sort is intended for players aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children and is not enrolled in Google Play’s Designed for Families program.

Information we collect

The game stores progress on the device (unlocked levels, coins, settings, daily streak) using Android local storage. That data does not leave the device by itself.

If you watch ads, Google AdMob (and, later, mediation partners such as AppLovin) may collect:

  • Advertising ID / device identifiers
  • IP address, device type, language, approximate location derived from IP
  • Ad interaction events (impressions, clicks, rewarded completions)

We do not create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email, or phone number in v1.

Why we use it

  • To save your game progress on this device
  • To show rewarded and interstitial ads that fund a free game
  • To measure whether ads were shown or completed

Ads and third parties

Ad serving is handled by Google AdMob. See Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob help. You can reset or opt out of personalized ads in Android settings (Google → Ads).

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through ads, contact us and we will delete what we control and disable ads on that install where possible.

Data retention

Local save data stays on your device until you clear app storage or uninstall. Advertising partners retain data according to their own policies.

Your choices

  • You can play without watching optional rewarded ads (undo/continue/extra slot will be limited).
  • You can opt out of ad personalization in device settings.
  • Uninstalling the app removes local progress.
