## Full description (4000)

Sort candy as it rolls down the factory belt. Tap a piece to send it into the matching box — fill the order before the belt jams.

Candy Belt Sort is a one-finger color puzzle. Easy to learn in three taps, then the factory speeds up across 10 worlds and 120 levels.

HOW TO PLAY
• Candies travel on the conveyor
• Tap to drop them into the open box of the same color
• A box needs 3 matching candies, then it seals and slides away
• Frozen pieces thaw, wrappers hide flavors, bombs must be flicked off the belt
• Fill the quota before anything falls off the end

WORLDS
Sugar Bakery, Gummy Lab, Chocolate Mill, Cotton Cloud, Berry Factory, Mint Works, Lollipop Lane, Caramel Core, Rainbow Mix, Midnight Kettle

FREE TO PLAY
Optional rewarded videos for an extra box slot, undo, or a continue after a jam. Short ads between some levels. You can play without buying anything.
